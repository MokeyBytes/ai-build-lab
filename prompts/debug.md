# Debug one problem

Help me diagnose this problem at my current skill level. Ask for missing evidence before proposing a fix.

Collect:

- My operating system, desktop AI app, and shell if commands are involved.
- The lesson and step I am on.
- What I expected and what actually happened.
- The exact error text, with credentials and personal data removed.
- The relevant file or recent diff.

Explain your leading hypothesis and give one small check that would support or rule it out. Wait for its result. After two failed attempts, reassess the evidence and the hypothesis.

For version-dependent behaviour, compare the installed version with current official documentation and release notes. Cite the supporting page. If you cannot access it, ask me for the relevant text rather than claiming a current check.

Once the cause is supported, propose the smallest fix, explain each changed line, and give a check for the original behaviour plus anything the fix might affect. Separate observations from guesses. State which checks you actually ran.
